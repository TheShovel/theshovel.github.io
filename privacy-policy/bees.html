Bees!! Privacy Policy

Updated October 10, 2026

I am just a random person on the internet making games. I don't need your data!

Bees is a single player game. There is no account system, no sign in, and no server that knows who you are. I will never ask for personal or sensitive information.

No accounts, no tracking

Bees has no login, no user accounts, and no cloud saving. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any information about you, and we do not create or receive any advertising or analytics identifiers.

Local data / save files

Bees stores save data on your device only, such as your tutorial progress and settings / preferences. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it, and clearing the app's storage deletes it too.

Android permissions

Bees requests only two permissions:
- INTERNET so the game can load updates and content for the website version. The Android version does not need it for anything else.
- VIBRATE for the game's own haptics feedback.
We do not have access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, phone, or Bluetooth, and we never ask for them.

External links

The game has an in-game button that opens theshovel.rocks in your browser. That link leaves the app and goes to a site with its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for that site or any other site we link to.

Children

Bees is safe for all ages, and from a privacy perspective it is completely safe for children. Since we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect it from children either.
