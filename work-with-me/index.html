Rates & availability

My rate

€10 / day

€10 a day is cheap on purpose. This is a side hustle for me, not what I make a living from: I'm in college, and the money goes towards my hobbies and the extra things my parents can't provide while I'm studying.

A day is around 4 hours of work. Tell me what you need and I'll give you a rough idea of how many days it'll take before we start, so there are no surprises.

Availability

I'm still a student, so I can't be around all the time. School and exams come first. I usually work on projects in the evenings and on weekends, and I'll always tell you honestly how much time I can give you before we begin. I'm on Romania time (Bucharest, UTC+2 in winter and UTC+3 in summer).

Payment

You pay 50% of the estimated total before I start, and the other 50% when the project is finished. If it takes fewer days than I estimated, I'll take the difference off the final payment. If it takes me longer than estimated, I won't charge you extra.

My stance on AI

I sometimes use AI tools while I work. They are not required for me to do my work, but they do speed things up. If a project requires me to not use AI, I'll follow that completely. If you need proof of that, I can provide it too during development of the application.
